# Hi, I'm Emma Claire Bennett

> A college student who loves both code and creativity — programming during the day, books, photography, and coffee-shop wandering when she logs off.

## About Me

Hi, I'm Emma.

I'm a college student studying computer science, and I'm fascinated by the process of turning an idea into something that actually works.

My interest in programming started with simple curiosity. I wanted to understand how the websites, apps, and tools I used every day were actually built.

That curiosity eventually turned into writing code.

Now I enjoy experimenting with different languages and technologies, building small projects, solving programming problems, and learning about the ideas behind the software we use every day.

I'm particularly interested in Python, web development, artificial intelligence, automation, and the intersection between technology and creativity.

I like projects that begin with a simple question:

> **“What if I built this?”**

Sometimes the result is useful. Sometimes it's completely unnecessary. Sometimes it doesn't work at all. But I've learned that the process of figuring out why something doesn't work can be just as interesting as building it in the first place.

Computer science is only one part of who I am, though.

When I'm not coding, I love photography, books, writing, coffee shops, music, traveling, and exploring new places. I can spend an afternoon debugging a program and then spend the evening reading a novel.

I like that combination.

Technology gives me a way to build things, while photography and writing give me a way to pay attention to the world around me.

I'm also interested in how technology affects the way people communicate, learn, create, and connect.
